SADRIA is a Copenhagen-based unisex jewelry brand, drawing on equestrian culture, precious metals and a sense of quiet strength. I worked across the brand’s visual direction and communication, shaping how its world comes together across imagery, social and graphic design. This included planning and art directing photoshoots and campaigns, developing the social media grid, creating graphic assets and refining the visual language across different brand touchpoints. The work was about building a visual world that feels as distinctive as the jewelry itself, combining the restraint of fine jewelry with a darker, cinematic sensibility, where equestrian references, landscape and the unexpected could exist side by side.
