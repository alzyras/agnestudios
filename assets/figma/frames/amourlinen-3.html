about

AmourLinen is a Lithuanian linen clothing brand, where I worked across the brand’s visual direction and digital presence as it moved towards a more refined and premium positioning.

​

I was responsible for the website design and development of the brand’s visual direction, working across e-commerce, social and campaign touchpoints to create a more cohesive visual language. Alongside the website, I designed static Meta advertising, email campaigns and newsletters, printed materials and other brand assets, while also assisting with image direction and contributing to the brand’s photography.

​

The work sat between brand development and day-to-day creative execution — building a stronger visual foundation while making sure it translated consistently across the places customers encounter the brand, from the website and advertising to email, social and print.
