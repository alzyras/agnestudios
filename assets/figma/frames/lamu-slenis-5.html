Lamų Slėnis is a Lithuanian print and digital magazine centred around slow living, thoughtful design and stories that are made to stay with you. I’ve been responsible for the editorial layout across several issues, translating each issue’s varied stories, interviews and visual material into a cohesive reading experience. The work spans typography, image selection and placement, grids and page composition, with each story given its own visual rhythm while remaining part of the wider magazine. Working across multiple issues has also meant developing and maintaining a consistent editorial language while adapting it to different subjects, imagery and formats.
