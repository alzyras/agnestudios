At 14, Andersen left Odense for Copenhagen, dreaming of becoming an actor. With little more than hope in his pocket, he faced the harsh realities of rejection. ​

“Rejection only sharpens the resolve to be heard,” he penned in his diary. The vibrant city sparked his creativity, laying the foundation for the fairy tales to come.
