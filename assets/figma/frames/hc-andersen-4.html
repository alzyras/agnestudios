Hans Christian Andersen (1805-1875) was a writer who left a lasting mark on storytelling. From The Little Mermaid to The Ugly Duckling, his fairy tales speak to themes of identity, change, and resilience.

​

Andersen’s stories, often filled with a quiet melancholy, explore the tension between fantasy and the real world. His work continues to resonate today, reminding us that imagination is not an escape but a way to understand the complexities of life.
