About Agne Studios

Mail agne.siupsinskaite@gmail.com Phone +370 655 87 436 Instagram agne.studios

I build visual identities and digital experiences for brands with a strong point of view.

I've been drawn to visual communication for as long as I can remember. What started as drawing everything I could as a child grew into a fascination with how colour, type, imagery and composition shape the way something is understood and felt.

I studied Publishing and Marketing at Vilnius University, then Multimedia Design in Copenhagen, where I developed my digital and web design practice. I later completed a Master's in Graphic & Visual Design at LABASAD, graduating with distinction.

Today, I work across brand identity, digital, editorial and content, helping fashion, beauty, lifestyle and culture-led brands create visual worlds that feel considered, distinctive and cohesive.

I've worked with both established and emerging brands, often at moments of growth or change: a new identity, a new website, a shift in positioning, a new collection, or simply the need for a brand to feel more like itself.

My work ranges from visual direction and web and e-commerce design to social and campaign content, video editing, email marketing and Meta advertising. Having worked on both the creative and the marketing side, I enjoy seeing how all the pieces connect, from the bigger visual idea to the details of how it shows up in the real world.

I'm interested in the space where strategy meets aesthetics: creating work that looks good, communicates clearly and makes people feel something.

Working on something, or thinking about it? I'd love to hear from you.
